Taste Over Technology

AI creates pixels; creative direction creates meaning. 35mm anamorphic glass, key-to-fill lighting ratios, and deliberate pacing must be directed with human taste from day one.

Rule 02

Continuity & Polish

Eliminating the uncanny AI sheen. Character face-mesh locking, consistent product materials, and optical flow tracking ensure seamless commercial cuts without morphing or distortion.

Traditional production takes months; raw AI generation lacks polish. We bridge both—delivering studio-grade commercial campaigns in days to let brands test and scale fast.

how we work together

THE PIPELINE
A disciplined 4-stage director's workflow from raw script to master color grade.
01

Story First

Concept & Treatment

We clarify the brand objective, audience hook, and narrative arc. I develop a comprehensive director's treatment outlining tone, camera grammar (35mm anamorphic, depth of field, color palette), and keyframe storyboards before generating a single video frame.

Milestone: Approved Treatment
02

Zero Uncanny Glitches

Continuity & Locks

I build locked character face-meshes, product geometry references, and lighting matrices. By establishing strict continuity locks across Midjourney, ComfyUI, and 3D reference passes, we eliminate visual morphing and maintain rock-solid brand fidelity.

Milestone: Locked Model Mesh
03

Directing the Motion

Generative Passes

Directing camera movement, speed ramps, and character kinetics using Runway Gen-3, Kling AI, and bespoke optical flow systems. Every shot is directed with cinematic intent rather than random generation luck.

Milestone: Selected Video Takes
04

The Film Comes Alive

Editorial & Sound

Raw generative passes are cut in Premiere Pro, color-graded in DaVinci Resolve, and layered with multi-track organic sound design, foley audio, and voiceover. The result is a tactile, broadcast-ready film delivered on schedule.

Milestone: Broadcast Master
